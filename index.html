Put it on your iPad home screen

  1. Open a subject above in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open it from its new icon. After that first visit it works with no internet.

Each subject is a separate app, so add the ones you study. When your teacher updates a game, it updates itself the next time you open it with Wi-Fi.
